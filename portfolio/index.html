About

Grew up in Annecy, studied design at ENSCI in Paris, moved to Lyon for a job that was supposed to last a year and never left. I started drawing icons for a startup and slowly shifted toward the kind of systemic product thinking where a button's label matters more than its drop shadow.

I'm strongest at the messy middle — the phase where you have real user feedback, a backlog that's too long, and stakeholders who each think a different feature is urgent. I bring clarity to that. Wireframes, testing, iteration, repeat until the support tickets stop saying "how do I…".
